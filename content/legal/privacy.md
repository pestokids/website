# Privacy policy

**Last updated:** {{LAST_UPDATED}}

Hi! We're Pesto. We make learning feel like play for kids aged 3 to 8. You're trusting us with information about your family, so here's exactly what we do with it, in plain words first and the full version after.

## The short version

- **We collect a little, and only from you, the parent.** On the website: your email, your kids' first names and ages, and your answers in the application. In the app: your WhatsApp number (no email) and, for each kid, a first name, age and a few details you choose.
- **We never sell your information,** and we never use anything about your kids for advertising. The app has no ads, analytics or tracking.
- **Grown-ups set things up.** Kids don't use this website, and in the app a parent or guardian enters every kid's details.
- **The app listens, but never records.** It notices when your kid speaks during "say it" moments, right on the device. Nothing is recorded, kept or sent.
- **We keep waitlist information for 12 months after the beta ends,** then delete it, unless your family joins.
- **Want it gone sooner?** For the waitlist, email privacy@pestokids.com from the address you applied with, and we'll delete it within 30 days. For the app, [delete your account](#delete-account) and everything is gone within 14 days.
- **Everything we store is in the United States.** The app's game progress stays on your device.

The "What this means" notes below are friendly summaries to help you read. The text under each one is the actual policy.

## 1. Who we are

*What this means: Pesto is a small team. This policy covers our website, everything around the Founding Families beta, and the Pesto app.*

This policy explains how Pesto ("we", "us") handles personal information when you visit pestokids.com, apply to the Founding Families program, receive our emails, message us on WhatsApp, or use the Pesto app for iOS and Android (together, the "Services"). Sections 2 to 6 cover the website and the waitlist, sections 7 and 8 cover the app, and the rest cover both.

## 2. What we collect

*What this means: only what we need to pick Founding Families and keep you posted. Nothing from your kids directly.*

| What | Examples | Where it comes from |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Your contact details | Your email address | You, on the application |
| About your kids | First names (a nickname is fine) and age bracket, for up to 2 kids | You, on the application |
| Your answers | What you hope Pesto helps with, what your kids do after school | You, on the application |
| How you found us | The link or ad you came from, campaign tags, the referring site | Your browser, when you arrive |
| How the site is used | Pages and steps viewed, buttons tapped, errors, device and browser type | Your browser, through our analytics |
| WhatsApp chats | Your phone number and the messages you send us | You, if you message us |

We give each application a short code (for example PF-7K2Q) to match your WhatsApp message to your application.

**What we don't collect:** we don't ask for your kids' last names, photos, voices, school, precise location, or anything else, and kids never type into our website. We don't collect payment details, because the beta is free.

## 3. How we use it

*What this means: to run the waitlist, talk to you, and see which of our ads and pages actually work.*

We use your information to:

- review applications and choose Founding Families;
- send you your confirmation, your Founding Family letter and beta updates;
- reply to you on WhatsApp, including the story we promise for your kids;
- personalise what you see (for example, using your kids' first names in the application and your emails);
- understand which ads, links and pages bring families to us, and improve the site;
- keep the Services safe, for example by blocking spam and fake sign-ups.

We never use information about your kids for advertising, and we never build advertising profiles of children.

## 4. Who we share it with

*What this means: a short list of companies that help us run the site. They work for us and can't use your information for themselves. We never sell it.*

We don't sell personal information. We share it only with service providers who process it on our behalf, under contracts that limit them to providing their service to us:

| Provider | What they do for us | What they receive |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Vercel | Hosts the website | Technical data when you visit |
| Convex | Stores applications (our database) | Everything in your application |
| Resend | Sends our emails | Your email address, kids' first names, email content |
| Google Workspace | Our team inbox | Emails you send us |
| PostHog | Website analytics and error reports | Usage events, device data, your email once you apply |
| Linkrunner | Measures which ads and links bring families to us | Visit and sign-up events, your email |
| Meta (Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp) | Ad measurement; WhatsApp messaging | Whether someone who clicked our ad applied (never your email); your WhatsApp messages |

We never send your kids' names or ages to Linkrunner or Meta. We may also share information if the law requires it, to protect people's safety, or if Pesto is merged or acquired. Any new owner must keep the promises in this policy.

## 5. Cookies and similar tech

*What this means: your browser remembers your application progress, and our analytics remembers your visit. No ads are shown on our site.*

Our site saves your application progress in your browser so you can pick up where you left off. PostHog and Linkrunner use browser storage to recognise repeat visits and measure our ads. We don't show ads on our website.

## 6. How long we keep it

*What this means: about a year after the beta, unless you join.*

We keep waitlist information until 12 months after the Founding Families beta ends, then delete it, unless your family joins Pesto, in which case section 7 covers the app. Analytics and service logs are kept for our providers' standard periods. We delete sooner when you ask.

## 7. The Pesto app

*What this means: the app is for kids aged 3 to 8, on iOS and Android. You sign in with WhatsApp, you tell us a little about each kid, and that's it. No ads, no analytics, no tracking.*

The Pesto app is a learning game for kids aged 3 to 8, for iOS and Android. Kids play it under a parent or guardian's account. This is what the app collects:

| What | Details | Where it comes from |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Your WhatsApp number | Used to sign you in. The app doesn't ask for your email. | You, when you sign in |
| Your parent PIN | Stored hashed, so no one can read it, including us | You, when you set it |
| About each kid, for up to 4 kids | First name, age, family language, interests, after-school activities and avatar | You, a parent or guardian, in the app. Kids don't enter these. |
| About the device | A random install ID, the device model and OS version, and when the app was last opened | The app |

**How we use it.** We use your WhatsApp number to sign you in; sign-in codes are sent to you through our WhatsApp messaging provider. We use your kids' details to set up the app for each kid, so the stories and games fit their age, language and interests. We use the device details to keep the app working on your device and to help when something goes wrong.

**Story audio with your kid's name.** We use your kid's first name to make story audio that says their name. Service providers help us make it and process the name only on our behalf, under contracts that limit them to providing their service to us.

**The microphone.** The app asks to use the microphone only to notice when your kid speaks during "say it" moments in the game. This is measured on the device. Nothing is recorded, kept or sent, to us or to anyone else. You can turn off microphone access in your device's settings at any time.

**Game progress.** Your kids' progress in the game is stored on the device only. We don't keep it on our servers.

**What the app never does.** The app has no ads, no analytics and no tracking. We don't sell your information or share it for advertising, and the app doesn't use your device's advertising ID.

**Who helps us.** We share app information only with service providers who process it on our behalf: to store it, to send sign-in codes on WhatsApp and to make story audio. Their contracts limit them to providing their service to us. We may also share information if the law requires it, to protect people's safety, or if Pesto is merged or acquired, as described in section 4.

**How long we keep it.** We keep your account and your kids' profiles while your account is open. When you delete your account, everything is deleted within 14 days (see section 8).

## 8. Deleting your account {#delete-account}

*What this means: delete your account from the app or by email, and your account and every kid's profile are gone within 14 days.*

You can delete your Pesto app account at any time, in either of these ways:

- **In the app:** open Parents' corner and tap Delete account.
- **By email:** email privacy@pestokids.com and tell us the WhatsApp number on the account. We only act on a request from that number's owner, so we may message the number to check it's you.

Your account, every kid's profile and their progress are deleted within 14 days. Game progress is stored only on the device, so deleting the app removes it too. To delete your waitlist information from the website, see section 9.

## 9. Your choices and rights

*What this means: you can see, fix or delete your information anytime, wherever you live.*

You can ask us to show you, correct or delete the information we hold about you and your kids, or to stop using it. For the website and the waitlist, email privacy@pestokids.com from the address you applied with, so we know it's you, and we'll respond within 30 days. For the app, you can see and change your kids' details in Parents' corner, delete your account as described in section 8, or email privacy@pestokids.com with the WhatsApp number on your account. We give these rights to everyone, not only to residents of states whose laws require them, and we won't treat you differently for using them.

## 10. Children's privacy

*What this means: the website is for grown-ups. The app is for kids, but a grown-up sets it up and enters every detail, and can change or delete them anytime.*

The website and the Founding Families program are for parents and guardians aged 18 or older. The Pesto app is for kids aged 3 to 8 to play, under a parent or guardian's account. A parent or guardian signs in and enters each kid's details; kids don't. We collect only the kid details listed in section 7, we use them only to run Pesto for your family, and we never ask a kid for more information than they need to play.

As a parent or guardian, you can:

- **review and change** your kids' details at any time, in the app's Parents' corner;
- **delete** a kid's details, or your whole account, in Parents' corner or by emailing privacy@pestokids.com;
- **ask us to stop** collecting or using your kids' information, by deleting their profile or your account.

These are the rights the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) gives parents, and we give them to every family. We don't knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us information, email privacy@pestokids.com and we'll delete it.

## 11. Keeping it safe

*What this means: we use well-run services and limit who can see your data. No system is perfect, and we'll tell you if something goes wrong.*

We use reasonable safeguards, including encrypted connections, access limited to the people who need it, providers with strong security practices, and storing your parent PIN only in hashed form. If a breach affects your information, we'll notify you as the law requires.

## 12. Where your data lives

*What this means: in the United States, except game progress, which stays on your device.*

Our servers and our providers' systems that store your information are in the United States. The app's game progress is stored only on your device.

## 13. Changes to this policy

*What this means: we'll tell you before anything big changes.*

If we make a material change, we'll tell you before it takes effect, by email or on WhatsApp. We won't use information about your kids in a new way without asking you first. The date at the top shows the latest version.
