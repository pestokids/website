# Privacy policy

**Last updated:** {{LAST_UPDATED}}

Hi! We're Pesto. We make learning feel like play for kids aged 3 to 8. You're trusting us with information about your family, so here's exactly what we do with it, in plain words first and the full version after.

## The short version

- **We collect a little, from you, the parent:** your email, your kids' first names and ages, and your answers in the application.
- **We never sell your information,** and we never use anything about your kids for advertising.
- **Kids don't use this website.** Everything here comes from you, a parent or guardian.
- **We keep waitlist information for 12 months after the beta ends,** then delete it, unless your family joins.
- **Want it gone sooner?** Email privacy@pestokids.com from the address you applied with, and we'll delete it within 30 days.
- **Everything is stored in the United States.**

The "What this means" notes below are friendly summaries to help you read. The text under each one is the actual policy.

## 1. Who we are

*What this means: Pesto is a small team. This policy covers our website and everything around the Founding Families beta.*

This policy explains how Plus Nine One, Inc., a Delaware corporation doing business as Pesto ("Pesto", "we", "us") handles personal information when you visit pestokids.com, apply to the Founding Families program, receive our emails, or message us on WhatsApp (together, the "Services"). It does not yet cover the Pesto app for kids. Before any child uses the app, we will publish an app privacy section and ask for your consent as a parent.

## 2. What we collect

*What this means: only what we need to pick Founding Families and keep you posted. Nothing from your kids directly.*

| What | Examples | Where it comes from |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Your contact details | Your email address | You, on the application |
| About your kids | First names (a nickname is fine) and age bracket, for up to 2 kids | You, on the application |
| Your answers | What you hope Pesto helps with, what your kids do after school | You, on the application |
| How you found us | The link or ad you came from, campaign tags, the referring site | Your browser, when you arrive |
| How the site is used | Pages and steps viewed, buttons tapped, errors, device and browser type | Your browser, through our analytics |
| WhatsApp chats | Your phone number and the messages you send us | You, if you message us |

We give each application a short code (for example PF-7K2Q) to match your WhatsApp message to your application.

**What we don't collect:** we don't ask for your kids' last names, photos, voices, school, precise location, or anything else, and kids never type into our website. We don't collect payment details, because the beta is free.

## 3. How we use it

*What this means: to run the waitlist, talk to you, and see which of our ads and pages actually work.*

We use your information to:

- review applications and choose Founding Families;
- send you your confirmation, your Founding Family letter and beta updates;
- reply to you on WhatsApp, including the story we promise for your kids;
- personalise what you see (for example, using your kids' first names in the application and your emails);
- understand which ads, links and pages bring families to us, and improve the site;
- keep the Services safe, for example by blocking spam and fake sign-ups.

We never use information about your kids for advertising, and we never build advertising profiles of children.

## 4. Who we share it with

*What this means: a short list of companies that help us run the site. They work for us and can't use your information for themselves. We never sell it.*

We don't sell personal information. We share it only with service providers who process it on our behalf, under contracts that limit them to providing their service to us:

| Provider | What they do for us | What they receive |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Vercel | Hosts the website | Technical data when you visit |
| Convex | Stores applications (our database) | Everything in your application |
| Resend | Sends our emails | Your email address, kids' first names, email content |
| Google Workspace | Our team inbox | Emails you send us |
| PostHog | Website analytics and error reports | Usage events, device data, your email once you apply |
| Linkrunner | Measures which ads and links bring families to us | Visit and sign-up events, your email |
| Meta (Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp) | Ad measurement; WhatsApp messaging | Whether someone who clicked our ad applied (never your email); your WhatsApp messages |

We never send your kids' names or ages to Linkrunner or Meta. We may also share information if the law requires it, to protect people's safety, or if Pesto is merged or acquired. Any new owner must keep the promises in this policy.

## 5. Cookies and similar tech

*What this means: your browser remembers your application progress, and our analytics remembers your visit. No ads are shown on our site.*

Our site saves your application progress in your browser so you can pick up where you left off. PostHog and Linkrunner use browser storage to recognise repeat visits and measure our ads. We don't show ads on our website.

## 6. How long we keep it

*What this means: about a year after the beta, unless you join.*

We keep waitlist information until 12 months after the Founding Families beta ends, then delete it, unless your family joins Pesto, in which case our app policy applies. Analytics and service logs are kept for our providers' standard periods. We delete sooner when you ask.

## 7. Your choices and rights

*What this means: you can see, fix or delete your information anytime, wherever you live.*

You can ask us to show you, correct or delete the information we hold about you and your kids, or to stop using it. Email privacy@pestokids.com from the address you applied with, so we know it's you. We'll respond within 30 days. We give these rights to everyone, not only to residents of states whose laws require them, and we won't treat you differently for using them.

## 8. Children's privacy

*What this means: this website is for grown-ups. Your kids' details come only from you.*

The website and the Founding Families program are for parents and guardians aged 18 or older. We don't knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us information, email privacy@pestokids.com and we'll delete it. The Pesto app will follow the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), including verifiable parental consent, before any child uses it.

## 9. Keeping it safe

*What this means: we use well-run services and limit who can see your data. No system is perfect, and we'll tell you if something goes wrong.*

We use reasonable safeguards, including encrypted connections, access limited to the people who need it, and providers with strong security practices. If a breach affects your information, we'll notify you as the law requires.

## 10. Where your data lives

*What this means: in the United States.*

Our servers and our providers' systems that store your information are in the United States.

## 11. Changes to this policy

*What this means: we'll tell you before anything big changes.*

If we make a material change, we'll email you before it takes effect. We won't use information about your kids in a new way without asking you first. The date at the top shows the latest version.
